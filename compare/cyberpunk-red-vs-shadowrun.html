What Cyberpunk RED is

Cyberpunk d10 — Night City 2045

Cyberpunk RED is a cyberpunk tabletop RPG from R. Talsorian Games (2020), the fourth edition of the Interlock system. Set in 2045, after the 4th Corporate War — Night City's sky is red from nuclear fallout, and players are edgerunners surviving between corporations and the streets. d10 system with Lifepath character creation and the brutal Friday Night Firefight combat subsystem.

Pick it if
  • you want classic Night City cyberpunk: corporations, cyberware and style
  • your players enjoy rolling up a backstory on Lifepath tables
  • you want gunfights where armor is fragile and one shot can kill
Skip it if
  • your group wants heroes who shrug off gunfire
  • you want rules-light play with near-zero prep — CY_BORG is leaner
  • your players want to stack cyberware with no cost to their Humanity
Key mechanics
  • Interlock System (d10)
  • Friday Night Firefight
  • Lifepath
  • Cyberware

What Shadowrun is

Cyberpunk + fantasy d6-pool — magic meets the Matrix

Shadowrun is a tabletop RPG from Catalyst Game Labs (6th Edition, 2019), a unique hybrid of cyberpunk and fantasy. Set in 2080 (the Sixth World) — magic has returned to Earth, metahumans (elves, orks, trolls, dwarves) coexist with cyberware and the Matrix. d6 pool system: roll Attribute + Skill, count hits on 5s and 6s. Shadowrunners are deniable assets who take illegal jobs from corporations.

Pick it if
  • you want cyberpunk heists mixed with magic, elves and dragons
  • your group enjoys crunchy subsystems for hacking, magic and rigging
  • you want deniable runners taking shady corporate jobs
Skip it if
  • you want a light system you can learn in one evening
  • your group dislikes juggling a separate subsystem for each character type
Key mechanics
  • d6 Pool
  • Edge
  • Four Layers
  • Metahumans
