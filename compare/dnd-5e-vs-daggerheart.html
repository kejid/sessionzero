What Dungeons & Dragons 5e is

The world's most popular d20 RPG

Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition is the fifth and most popular edition of the world's first tabletop roleplaying game, released in 2014. A d20 system with the elegant advantage/disadvantage mechanic, 12 classes, 13 ancestries, hundreds of spells and monsters. The SRD is free under Creative Commons. The game that brought RPGs back into pop culture through Critical Role, Stranger Things, and Baldur's Gate 3.

Key mechanics
  • Advantage / Disadvantage
  • Bounded Accuracy
  • Classes & Subclasses
  • Short & Long Rest

What Daggerheart is

Narrative fantasy by Darrington Press (Critical Role)

A narrative heroic fantasy by Darrington Press, the publishing arm of Critical Role. At its core: two d12s — a Hope die and a Fear die. The sum decides success; whichever rolled higher sets the tone — triumph or cost. A card-based class and ancestry system builds your hero like a deck, combat feels cinematic, and the GM banks Fear to twist the scene at the most dramatic moment.

Key mechanics
  • Hope & Fear dice
  • Card-built heroes
  • Cinematic combat
  • Stress & damage
