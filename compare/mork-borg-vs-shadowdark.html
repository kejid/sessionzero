What MÖRK BORG is

doom-metal art-punk fantasy RPG

An art-punk RPG about the final days of a dying world. The rules fit on a few pages, but the book is a work of art: acid colors, deranged layout, black metal atmosphere. Every session could be your last — literally, because the world ends on a schedule.

Pick it if
  • you want a grim, metal-soaked game where characters die fast
  • your group laughs off death and rolls a new character in minutes
  • you like a world that literally ends on a die-rolled schedule
Skip it if
  • your players get attached to characters and hate random deaths
  • you want long-term campaigns with heroic growth
  • you want pirates and ship battles in the same grim style (see Pirate Borg)
Key mechanics
  • Calendar of Miseries
  • Art as gameplay
  • Everything is random
  • Ecosystem

What Shadowdark is

OSR with real-time torches

A bridge between OSR and modern RPGs. Uses familiar d20 mechanics (attributes, AC, saves) but with old-school lethality and sense of danger. The most successful RPG Kickstarter in recent years. If D&D feels bloated — Shadowdark is the cure.

Pick it if
  • you want deadly dungeon crawls where the torch burns in real time
  • your players know D&D and want old-school danger without new rules
  • you want characters ready to play in five minutes
Skip it if
  • your players love planning character builds in advance
  • your group dislikes a real-world clock pressuring every decision
Key mechanics
  • Real-time torch timer
  • Random leveling
  • D&D compatibility
  • Easy to learn
