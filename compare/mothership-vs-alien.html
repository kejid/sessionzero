What Mothership is

Alien-style sci-fi horror RPG

Sci-fi horror on percentile dice. You are workers, scientists, and marines on spaceships where everything goes wrong. The stress and panic system makes every situation unpredictable. Modular structure: dozens of brilliant community scenarios. Character creation — 10 minutes.

Pick it if
  • you want Alien-style sci-fi horror with workers, not heroes
  • your group enjoys stress building into panic that can wreck a plan
  • you want short scenarios for one-shots and mini-campaigns
Skip it if
  • you want space opera with heroic crews and big battles
  • your players hate losing control of their character to a panic roll
  • you want a long campaign with steady character progression
Key mechanics
  • Panic and stress
  • 4 classes
  • One-page sheet
  • Modules

What ALIEN RPG is

Year Zero sci-fi horror RPG

The official RPG set in the Alien universe. Year Zero Engine with a unique stress mechanic: the more you panic, the more dice you roll. More dice = higher chance of success, BUT also higher chance of a panic attack. Two modes: campaign (long-form space game) and cinematic (one-shot horror, just like the movies).

Pick it if
  • you want cinematic sci-fi horror one-shots with pregens and secret agendas
  • your group enjoys panic, betrayal and characters who may not survive
  • fans of the films want official lore across the whole franchise
Skip it if
  • your players want to win fights against the monsters
  • your table hates hidden agendas turning players against each other
  • you want heroic space adventure rather than dread and survival
Key mechanics
  • Stress mechanic
  • Cinematic mode
  • Hidden agendas
  • Campaign mode
