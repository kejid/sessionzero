What Shadow of the Demon Lord is

Horror fantasy at the end of the world

Grim horror fantasy from D&D veteran Robert J. Schwalb. A d20 engine stripped to the wiring and rebuilt: d6 boons and banes instead of stacks of modifiers, fast and slow turns, Insanity and Corruption. Characters, rules, bestiary and a region of the world all fit in one book. Campaigns are short and brutal: level 0 to level 10 in about eleven sessions.

Key mechanics
  • Boons and banes on d6
  • Fast and slow turns
  • Insanity
  • Corruption

What Shadow of the Weird Wizard is

Bright heroic fantasy from the Demon Lord designer

The brighter sibling of Shadow of the Demon Lord: the same fast d20 engine with d6 boons and banes, minus the apocalypse and the gross-out. Characters are real heroes who start competent and grow quest by quest. Three paths stack into a class of your own, and a whole campaign fits into about ten short quests.

Key mechanics
  • Boons and banes on d6
  • Three paths, one hero
  • Monsters go first
  • Quests, not endless campaigns
