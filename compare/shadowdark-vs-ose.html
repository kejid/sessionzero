What Shadowdark is

OSR with real-time torches

A bridge between OSR and modern RPGs. Uses familiar d20 mechanics (attributes, AC, saves) but with old-school lethality and sense of danger. The most successful RPG Kickstarter in recent years. If D&D feels bloated — Shadowdark is the cure.

Pick it if
  • you want deadly dungeon crawls where the torch burns in real time
  • your players know D&D and want old-school danger without new rules
  • you want characters ready to play in five minutes
Skip it if
  • your players love planning character builds in advance
  • your group dislikes a real-world clock pressuring every decision
Key mechanics
  • Real-time torch timer
  • Random leveling
  • D&D compatibility
  • Easy to learn

What Old-School Essentials is

B/X retroclone OSR

Old-School Essentials is the gold-standard retroclone of the 1981 B/X Dungeons & Dragons rules. Designer Gavin Norman took those rules and made them even clearer: reorganized, cross-referenced, and laid out so precisely that any rule you need is findable in seconds. This is not a nostalgia trip. It's the best version of a brilliantly simple game that was already perfect.

Pick it if
  • you want classic B/X D&D with rules you can find in seconds
  • your group enjoys torch-tracking dungeon crawls and reaction rolls
  • you want to run decades of old-school modules with little conversion
Skip it if
  • you want the extra detail of 1e AD&D like weapon speeds (see OSRIC)
  • your players want rich character builds and feats
  • you want a modern rule set with story mechanics
Key mechanics
  • Dungeon Procedures
  • Reaction & Morale Rolls
  • Ascending & Descending AC
  • Modular Design
