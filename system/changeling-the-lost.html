What is this system

Changeling: The Lost is fairy-tale horror from Onyx Path's Chronicles of Darkness line. You play changelings: people stolen by the True Fae, dragged to Arcadia and remade into servants, toys and monsters — who managed to escape. The second edition (2019) runs on the Storytelling System: d10 pools, success on 8+. Clarity stands in for sanity, Contracts for fae magic, and oaths bind for real. It is a game about surviving abuse, so a session zero on boundaries matters here. Onyx Path called The Hedge (2024) the line's final scheduled project.

Is it for your table?
Pick it if
  • you want dark fairy-tale horror about surviving and escaping abuse
  • your group enjoys courtly politics, binding oaths and fae bargains
  • players want deep personal stories about reclaiming a stolen life
Skip it if
  • your table is not ready for heavy trauma themes, even with safety tools
  • you want light, whimsical faerie adventure
  • you want a GM with little prep; a chronicle takes real setup
Setting

Our world, with the Hedge growing behind it — a thorny maze of dreams and briars between reality and Arcadia. Coming home, a changeling finds their place taken by a Fetch, a fake copy left by their Keeper. The escaped hide their inhuman nature behind the Mask and gather into the Courts of Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter, each with its own way of living with what happened. And the Keepers' Huntsmen are on their trail.

What it looks like at the table
You run through the Hedge while a Huntsman's horn sounds behind you. The Storyteller calls for Dexterity 3 + Athletics 2: five dice — 3, 8, 10, 6, 9. The 10 rolls again — 4. Three successes, and you slip into a Hollow. But in a murky pool you glimpse your Keeper's face, and it's a breaking point. You mark Clarity damage — and think of your Touchstone, your sister, who still lives with your Fetch.

Key mechanics

d10 pools (Storytelling)

Roll Attribute + Skill in d10s; every 8+ is a success, 10s roll again, and five successes make an exceptional success. The same base as Chronicles of Darkness.

Clarity

A track like Health, but for your grip on reality, with a maximum of Wits + Composure. Trauma, fae magic and brushes with your Keeper deal Clarity damage, while Touchstones to your old life keep you anchored.

Pledges and Contracts

Changelings swear pledges that work literally: break one and the Wyrd punishes you. Contracts are the power of fae bargains — Royal, Common and risky Goblin Contracts — fuelled by Glamour.

Seemings and Courts

Six Seemings — Beast, Darkling, Elemental, Fairest, Ogre, Wizened — describe what you were made into. The seasonal Courts set your politics and your way of living with trauma.
