What is this system

Exalted is epic fantasy from Onyx Path. You play the Exalted: mortals touched by a shard of divine power that lets them cleave mountains and bargain with gods. The Third Edition (2016) is a 650+ page book: d10 pools where 7+ succeeds and 10s count double, initiative-driven combat instead of hit-point attrition, and hundreds of Charms that build into a personal fighting style. The setting, Creation, mixes wuxia, anime and myth on a vast scale. If Ex3 feels heavy, Exalted: Essence is the lighter one-book core where any kind of Exalt is playable.

Is it for your table?
Pick it if
  • you want epic demigods who cleave mountains and bargain with gods
  • your players love describing flashy stunts and getting dice for it
  • your group enjoys mastering a big, crunchy combat system with hundreds of Charms
Skip it if
  • you want something learnable in one evening — start with Exalted: Essence instead
  • your group prefers grounded, low-power heroes who struggle to survive
Setting

Creation is a flat world ringed by the chaos of the Wyld and the shadowlands of the dead. Fifteen centuries ago the Dragon-Blooded overthrew the Solar Exalted and branded them demons. Now the Scarlet Empress who ruled the Realm has vanished, her empire is cracking, and the Solars are returning — with the Wyld Hunt already on their trail. Silk palaces, First Age ruins, Deathlords and fallen gods wait for anyone bold enough to seize the world.

What it looks like at the table
A burning palanquin tips into the gorge and your Dawn Caste Solar leaps from it straight at the general. The table loves the description: a two-point stunt, +2 dice and a Willpower back. You sit at 14 Initiative — time for a decisive attack. Dexterity 5 + Melee 5, plus 2 stunt dice and 4 more from your Excellency for 4 motes of Essence: 16 dice against Defense 4. Successes to spare. Damage is your Initiative itself: roll 14 dice, each success a level of damage with no soak. The general falls, and your Initiative resets to 3.

Key mechanics

d10 pools: 7+ and double 10s

Roll Attribute + Ability in d10s. Every 7+ is a success and 10s count twice. Stunts — vivid descriptions of an action — add +2 dice, and the better ones restore Willpower too, so the table keeps raising the stakes.

Initiative combat

Withering attacks strip Initiative from a foe and add it to yours. A decisive attack rolls your banked Initiative as damage, then resets you to 3. Drop an enemy to zero Initiative or below and they crash.

Charms and Essence

Hundreds of Charms tied to Abilities, from swordplay that scatters armies to crafting and court intrigue. They burn motes of Essence, and spending big lights up an anima banner seen for miles.

Ex3 or Essence

Exalted Third Edition is the full, crunchy game, with separate books for other types of Exalt. Exalted: Essence is the lighter one-book core for any Exalt; its crowdfunded Player's Guide and Storyteller's Guide expansions are on the way.
