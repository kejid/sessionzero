What is this system

Fiasco is a tabletop RPG from Bully Pulpit Games (2nd Edition, 2021), inspired by the films of the Coen Brothers. GM-less, zero prep, d6 pool (Classic) or playing cards (2E). Players create characters through a Playset (theme/location), linked by relationships, and play through 3 acts: Setup, Tilt, Aftermath. Each roll is either something good (evens) or something bad (odds). The result is always a tragicomedy.

Setting

Setting-less. Each session is a new Playset: 1930s mobsters, a space station, a teenage camp, a medieval village. 500+ playsets at fiascoplaysets.com.

What it looks like at the table
Setup: Two brothers, owners of an abandoned farm. Relationship: 'One of us is a thief.' Tilt: A strange man with an axe pounds on the door. Aftermath: The farm burns. One brother is dead. The other is in prison. The third owns the farm — but the farm is gone. Fiasco.

Key mechanics

d6 Pool (Classic)

4d6 per player in two colors. Even = something good, odd = something bad. Doubles = disaster.

Three Acts

Setup (needs and relationships), Tilt (turns and complications), Aftermath (fallout). Each act is 2-3 rounds of rolls.

Playsets

Session themes: mob, space, teens, medieval. Each playset has tables for Needs, Locations, Objects.

No GM

Everyone plays. Everyone decides. Everyone suffers. Fiasco is about all of you.
