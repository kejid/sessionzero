What is this system

Grimwild (2024) is a heroic fantasy RPG by J. D. Maxwell and Oddity Press: familiar classes like the fighter, rogue and wizard, run on a Blades in the Dark-style engine. Roll a pool of d6, read the highest die, and let the GM add d8 'thorns' for difficulty. The CC BY 4.0 Free Edition won the 2025 gold ENnie for Best Free Product. The designer went silent in 2025, and the community has since released a free Community Edition.

Setting

No fixed setting: classic adventure fantasy with dungeons, ruins and wild country. The party and GM draw the region, settlements and even dungeons together during play, as nodes and paths in a pointcrawl. Published modules and other settings port in easily.

What it looks like at the table
The rogue climbs the tower wall before the guards turn around. Agility 2 plus one spark — 3d6. The stones are slick, so the GM adds one d8 thorn for difficulty. The dice show 6, 3, 2 — perfect… but the thorn lands on 8 and cuts it to messy. The rogue reaches the roof, and the GM makes an impact move: a tile clatters into the courtyard. The GM rolls the 4d6 'Alarm' pool: 1, 5, 2, 6 — the two dice under 4 drop out, two remain. When the pool is empty, the guards arrive.

Key mechanics

Perfect, messy, grim

Roll a d6 pool and keep only the highest die: 6 is perfect (clean success), 4–5 is messy (success with trouble), 1–3 is grim (failure with trouble). Two or more 6s is a critical, which can't be cut.

Thorns

Difficulty isn't a penalty to your dice — it's d8 thorns added to the pool. A 7 or 8 on a thorn cuts the result one step. A grim result cut by a thorn becomes a disaster, and the player gains spark.

Diminishing pools

Clocks are replaced by pools of d6. When an event advances, the GM rolls the pool and removes every die showing 1–3. When the pool is empty, something happens: the guards show up, the bridge collapses, an ability runs dry.

Spark and story

Spark is protagonist fuel: each one adds +1d to a roll, earned from disasters and resolved story arcs. Twice per session a player can add a detail about their character to the scene, and the party expands the world map with shared tokens.
