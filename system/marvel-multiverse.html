What is this system

Marvel Multiverse RPG is a tabletop RPG from Marvel (2023), set in the Marvel Multiverse (Earth-616). d616 system: roll 3d6 (the middle one is the Marvel Die) + ability vs a target number. The Marvel Die's 1 is an 'M' that counts as a 6; 6-M-6 is the Ultimate Fantastic Roll, an automatic success. About 130 pages of ready-made hero and villain profiles (Iron Man, Spider-Man, Avengers, X-Men). Free quickstart at marvel.com/rpg.

Is it for your table?
Pick it if
  • your group wants to play Spider-Man, Wolverine or other canon Marvel heroes
  • you like light dice math with a dramatic special die on every roll
  • you want ready-made hero profiles instead of building powers from scratch
Skip it if
  • you want to design any superhero power you can imagine point by point
  • your table prefers teen drama and identity over smashing villains
  • you want a world of your own with no Marvel canon attached
Setting

The Marvel Universe — Earth-616 and beyond. Avengers, X-Men, Guardians of the Galaxy — all in one catalog. Expansion books (such as the X-Men and Spider-Verse ones) add heroes, powers and locations.

What it looks like at the table
Iron Man fires a repulsor blast: 4, a 2 on the Marvel Die, 6 — that's 12, plus his Agility. The target number falls, and the blast tears through the gang. Had the Marvel Die come up 1, it would have been an 'M' and a Fantastic success. Welcome to the Multiverse, where even luck is a superpower.

Key mechanics

d616 System

Roll 3d6 (the middle one is the Marvel Die) + ability vs a target number. An 'M' on the Marvel Die counts as a 6, and 6-M-6 is the Ultimate Fantastic Roll, an automatic success. Simple but dramatic math.

Marvel Die

One die in every roll is the Marvel Die, with an 'M' in place of the 1. Its result is multiplied by your damage multiplier, and an 'M' gives a Fantastic success with special effects.

Ready-made Heroes

About 130 pages of the book are ready-made profiles: Iron Man, Spider-Man, Captain America, Wolverine, Doctor Strange, and dozens more. Each with unique powers.

Ranks (1–6)

Six power ranks, from street-level newcomers to cosmic powerhouses. Each rank brings new power levels and capabilities.
