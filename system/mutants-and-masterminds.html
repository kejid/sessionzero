What is this system

The best-known generic superhero RPG. A d20 engine with no classes and no hit points: heroes are built with power points, and every ability, from flight to mind control, is assembled from effects and modifiers. Power Level keeps the team balanced. Third edition (2011) is the current game; Green Ronin crowdfunded fourth edition on Kickstarter in early 2026 after a public playtest in 2025, with release planned for late 2026.

Is it for your table?
Pick it if
  • you want to build any superhero power from effects and modifiers
  • your group enjoys point-buy character design and tactical fights
  • you want one ruleset for street vigilantes and cosmic heroes alike
Skip it if
  • your players want to jump in without a long character build
  • you want teen drama over powers and combat
  • you want ready-made Marvel heroes instead of your own
Setting

Earth-Prime is a comic-book universe with decades of history: Freedom City and its legendary teams, Emerald City, secret societies, alien invasions and time travel. The setting is optional, though. The rules handle street-level vigilantes and cosmic gods equally well, so you can play in your own world or your take on a favorite series.

What it looks like at the table
The villain fires an energy blast with Damage 10, so the Toughness resistance DC is 15 + 10 = 25. Your hero has Toughness 10 and rolls a 9 on the d20: total 19, a miss by 6, which is two degrees of failure. She's dazed and takes −1 on further Toughness checks. The GM triggers her Secret Identity complication: a news photographer is filming from the street below. That complication earns a Hero Point, and you spend it to shake off the daze. You strike back: d20 + 12 attack against the villain's Dodge (10 + 8 = 18). You roll 11 — a hit.

Key mechanics

Effects-based powers

No spell lists. A fireball is the Damage effect with ranged and area modifiers; teleportation is Movement with the right extras. Any idea from the comics can be built and priced in points.

No hit points

A hit forces a Toughness check against 15 + damage rank. The worse you fail, the worse your condition: −1 Toughness, dazed, staggered, incapacitated. Fights stay short and cinematic.

Hero Points and complications

Hero Points buy rerolls, scene edits and instant recovery. You earn them when the GM invokes your complications: a secret identity, a responsibility, an arch-enemy.

Power Level

Power Level caps attack, defense and damage. The flying brick and the fragile sharpshooter end up in the same weight class: the team is balanced, yet every hero plays differently.
