What is this system

Grim horror fantasy from D&D veteran Robert J. Schwalb. A d20 engine stripped to the wiring and rebuilt: d6 boons and banes instead of stacks of modifiers, fast and slow turns, Insanity and Corruption. Characters, rules, bestiary and a region of the world all fit in one book. Campaigns are short and brutal: level 0 to level 10 in about eleven sessions.

Setting

Urth stands on the brink. The Demon Lord strains to break free of the Void, and its Shadow brings war, plague and ancient horrors: herds of beastmen, warped spirits loosed from the Underworld, demons spilling into the world. The world needs heroes, but in these last days it will take anyone it can get — heroes, blackguards and madmen alike. You can also run it as plain dark fantasy without the inevitable end.

What it looks like at the table
A demon tears free of the summoning circle. Just seeing it calls for a Will challenge roll: the d20 shows 6, Will 9 gives −1, total 5 against 10 — failure. You gain 1 Insanity, bringing you to 3, and you're frightened for 3 rounds. If your Insanity ever reaches your Will score, you go mad. Next round you take a fast turn to strike before the horrors act, but you only get one action. Strength attack: the d20 shows 14, plus 2, for 16. Frightened while the demon is in sight means 3 banes: the 3d6 show 3, 4 and 1, and only the highest counts, so −4. Total 12 against Defense 12 — a hit, just barely.

Key mechanics

Boons and banes on d6

d20 plus a modifier (score minus 10) against 10 for challenge rolls; attacks target Defense or one of the target's attributes. Boons and banes cancel one for one, the rest are rolled as d6s and only the highest counts.

Fast and slow turns

Each round runs in phases: player fast turns, enemy fast turns, then the slow turns. A fast turn gives you a single action but lets you beat the monsters to it; a slow turn gives you a move and an action. Speed or completeness, every round.

Insanity

Horrors, grievous wounds and the deaths of loved ones build Insanity. Each new point leaves you frightened for as many rounds as your Insanity total. When the total reaches your Will score, your character goes mad.

Corruption

Cold-blooded murder and learning forbidden traditions like Black Magic and Demonology stain the soul with Corruption. A few points and children cry, animals turn on you and food spoils at your touch. Power is there for the taking, but your soul pays for it.
