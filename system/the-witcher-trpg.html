What is this system

The official Witcher tabletop RPG from R. Talsorian Games, the makers of Cyberpunk, built in collaboration with CD Projekt Red. You don't have to play a witcher: bards, mages, merchants, craftsmen, doctors, criminals and men-at-arms share the road. Roll d10 + Stat + Skill, survive brutal fights with aimed strikes and critical wounds, brew and craft just like in the video games — and take contracts where the monster hunt always turns into something bigger.

Setting

The Continent in the thick of the war with Nilfgaard, between the events of the second and third games. The Northern Kingdoms claw at each other, sorcerers scheme, nonhumans live in ghettos or take to the woods as rebels, and drowners, ghouls and worse prowl the roads and swamps. It's a morally grey world: a monster contract easily becomes a story about human cruelty.

What it looks like at the table
Your witcher swings at a drowner: REF 8 + Swordsmanship 6 + d10. The die shows a 10 — it explodes, you roll again and add 4, for a total of 28. The drowner tries to dodge and gets 15. Beating its defense by 13 means a difficult critical wound. Roll d10 for hit location: 3, the torso, so damage lands at full value minus armor, plus the critical's bonus. Behind him, the mage pours more Stamina into a spell than her Vigor threshold allows — and every point over it tears into her own health.

Key mechanics

d10 + Stat + Skill

Every check is one d10 plus a Stat and a Skill against a Difficulty Value or an opposed roll. A 10 explodes and adds another roll; a 1 is a fumble that subtracts the next roll.

Aimed strikes and crits

Attacks land on specific body parts: the head takes triple damage, limbs take half. Beat the defense by 7 or more and you inflict a critical wound, from simple all the way to deadly.

Alchemy and crafting

Diagrams, formulae and ingredients straight out of the games. Craftsmen forge swords and armor, alchemists brew potions, oils and bombs, and monster loot becomes raw material.

Lifepath

Characters are built through background tables: family fate, friends, enemies, romances and the events of each decade of their life. Your hero arrives at the table already carrying debts and secrets.
