What is this system

Torchbearer is a harsh dungeon crawler from the makers of Burning Wheel and Mouse Guard, Thor Olavsrud and Luke Crane. The real enemy is not the dragon but hunger, darkness and fatigue: every turn drags the party toward a new condition, torches burn out, and your pack has room only for what truly matters. Tests use a pool of d6s where every 4–6 is a success, yet even failure pushes the story forward. The second edition, crowdfunded in 2020, is a two-book set: the Dungeoneer's Handbook and the Scholar's Guide.

Setting

Grim classic fantasy: forgotten tombs, ruined towers and dungeons packed with monsters and traps. The heroes are not chosen ones but desperate drifters who go underground because there is no other way to make a living. Between delves the party returns to town to spend its loot on lodging, food and gear, then heads back into the dark.

What it looks like at the table
Your burglar has one turn of light left and the chest is still locked. The GM sets Obstacle 3. Your Criminal skill is 3, so you roll three d6: 6, 4 and 1. Two successes, one short. Instead of a twist the GM grants success with a condition: the lid swings open, but now you're Exhausted. The turn ends, the torch dies, and the grind creeps toward the next condition.

Key mechanics

Light as a resource

Torches, lanterns and candles burn for a limited number of turns and light only a few characters. Whoever is left in the dark acts worse and risks more.

The grind

Every test in the dungeon takes a turn. Every fourth turn each adventurer takes a new condition, starting with Hungry and Thirsty and getting worse from there, all the way to Injured, Afraid and finally Dead. Time is the cruellest opponent.

Slot-based inventory

Pack, belt and hands each hold a fixed number of slots. Carry an extra sack and you can't carry your sword. Every piece of loot is a choice about what to leave behind.

Adventure, camp, town

Traits played against yourself earn checks. In camp each check buys one test — to recover, mend gear or prepare — and without checks you can't even catch your breath. In town the currency changes: lodging and shopping are paid for with a Lifestyle and Resources test on the way out.
